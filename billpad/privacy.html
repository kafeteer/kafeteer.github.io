BillpadPrivacy Policy

Last updated: May 7, 2025

At Billpad, we prioritize your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use our app.

1. Data Collection

We do not collect, store, or share any personal data. All information related to your transactions, including amounts, categories, and notes, is stored locally on your device and/or in your personal iCloud account if you have enabled iCloud syncing.

2. Local Storage

All data related to your payments is saved directly on your device’s local storage. This data is only accessible by you and remains private unless you choose to share it with others.

3. iCloud Integration

If you enable iCloud, your data will be synced across your Apple devices via your iCloud account. This process is managed by Apple, and we do not have access to any data stored in iCloud.

4. Third-Party Services

Billpad does not integrate with or send data to any third-party services, including advertising networks or analytics providers. Your data remains solely within your control.

5. Data Security

Since all data is stored locally on your device and/or in iCloud, we recommend ensuring that your device and iCloud account are properly secured with a password, Face ID, or Touch ID to prevent unauthorized access.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the “Last updated” date at the top of this page. Any changes will take effect immediately upon posting.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please contact us at:
